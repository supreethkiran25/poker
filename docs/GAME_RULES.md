# Texas Hold'em Game Rules Reference

## 1. Objective
Construct the highest-ranking 5-card poker hand using any combination of the player's 2 private hole cards and the 5 shared community cards.

---

## 2. Table Positions & Deal
- **Dealer (Button)**: Rotates clockwise each hand.
- **Small Blind (SB)**: Posted by the player immediately clockwise from the button.
- **Big Blind (BB)**: Posted by the player immediately clockwise from the SB (typically 2x SB).
- **Heads-Up Special Rule**:
  - The Dealer posts the Small Blind and acts first preflop.
  - The Big Blind acts second preflop.
  - On postflop betting rounds (flop, turn, river), the Big Blind acts first, and the Dealer acts second.

---

## 3. Betting Streets
1. **Preflop**: Each player receives 2 private cards. Action begins with the player to the left of the Big Blind.
2. **Flop**: 3 community cards dealt face up. Action begins with the first active player clockwise from the button.
3. **Turn**: 1 community card dealt face up. Action follows the same order.
4. **River**: 1 community card dealt face up. Final betting round.
5. **Showdown**: If two or more players remain, hands are evaluated and the pot is awarded to the best hand(s).

---

## 4. Betting Rules
- **Minimum Bet**: Equal to 1 Big Blind.
- **Minimum Raise**: Must be at least equal to the previous raise increment in the current betting round.
- **All-In**: A player may wager all remaining chips at any time. If this amount is less than the current bet or legal raise, a side pot is established for other players.
